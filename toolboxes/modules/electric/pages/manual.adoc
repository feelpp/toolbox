Welcome to the Electrostatics toolbox.

The Electrostatics toolbox solves a steady charge-conservation conduction model for the electric potential.
It is often coupled in our applications with the xref:toolbox:thermoelectric[Heat Transfer toolbox] to model Joule heating.
