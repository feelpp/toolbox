= Electric examples
:page-tags: examples
:description: Electric toolbox examples and feature coverage

This page summarizes the currently documented/available electric examples and what they cover.

== Cases

[options="header"]
|===
| Case | Path | Dimension | Highlights

| Quarter turn
| `toolboxes/electric/cases/quarter-turn`
| 2D, 3D
| Dirichlet potential BC and analytical benchmark on curved geometry.

| Busbar
| `toolboxes/electric/cases/busbar`
| 2D, 3D
| Dirichlet potential BC and analytical benchmark on rectangular geometry.

| Convergence square
| `toolboxes/electric/cases/convergence-square`
| 2D
| Exact solution with L2/H1 norm checks.

| Electromagnets (HL-31_H1 / HL-31-H1H4)
| `toolboxes/electric/cases/ElectroMagnets/*`
| 3D
| Industrial-like geometry, multiple material markers, and `ground` usage.
|===

== Boundary-condition coverage by examples

[options="header"]
|===
| Boundary condition (implementation) | Demonstrated by current cases

| `electric_potential` / `electric_potential_imposed`
| yes

| `ground`
| yes (`HL-31_H1`)

| `surface_charge_density` / `charge_density`
